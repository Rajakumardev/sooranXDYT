# Verdict — what the numbers say

_Appended by the agent after reading the tables above. (Re-running
`demand-check.mjs` regenerates the tables and drops this section.)_

## 1. The no-commentary solo lane is real and large

| Query | median views | min | no-commentary share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Battlefield REDSEC Solos no commentary` | **28,020** | 100 | 9 / 10 |
| `Battlefield REDSEC solo win no commentary` | **49,485** | 23,457 | 6 / 10 |
| `Battlefield 6 battle royale no commentary` (**past month**) | **14,863** | **5,965** | 7 / 10 |
| `battle royale solo no commentary` | **2,634,074** | 1,226,884 | 10 / 10 |
| `Delta Force Operations solo no commentary` | 32,881 | 119 | **3 / 10** |
| `Black Ops 7 zombies no commentary` | 8,824 | 4,121 | 9 / 10 |

Compare with **K1 = 6 views**. The format (solo BR, no commentary) is not the
problem — the game was. Fresh BF6 uploads in the last month still have a floor of
**~6,000 views**.

## 2. The honest small-channel floor (this is the number that matters)

Sub-3K channels in the REDSEC Solos query, all 21–22 min long:

| channel | subs | video | views | likes | like% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WanTeD GAMEPLAY | 629 | `Battlefield 6 Redsec: Solo Victory Gameplay (No Commentary)` | **362** | 13 | **3.59** |
| DeeV FPS | 2,720 | `Battlefield 6 RedSec 20 Kill Full Match PC WIN Gameplay (No Commentary)` | **347** | 9 | **2.59** |
| TAPFPS | **9** | `BATTLEFIELD Redsec SOLO WIN Gameplay (No Commentary)` | **100** | 2 | 2.00 |

So: **100–400 views is what a comparable no-name channel gets on this exact
format** — roughly 17–60× K1. The established lane (NoAnnoyingCommentary, 503K
subs) gets 23K–73K on the same format. Target the floor first; the ceiling is
proof the lane scales.

## 3. "WIN" is the strongest hook, and it beats "map/perk" framing

The win-framed query's median (49,485) is **1.8×** the general Solos median, and
the winners are explicit about it: Swagg "My FIRST SOLO WIN", Marshal "BATTLEFIELD
6 BATTLE ROYAL **WIN** GAMEPLAY", WanTeD "Solo **Victory** Gameplay", Threatty
"Battle Royale **Solo Win**". A first win is a genuine milestone → make the win
hunt the series arc, and never publish a result the VOD doesn't show.

## 4. The winning titles are plain, not clever

The two no-commentary leaders both use near-identical, keyword-first titles on
**every** upload — NoAnnoyingCommentary: `BATTLEFIELD 6 REDSEC BATTLE ROYALE
GAMEPLAY! (NO COMMENTARY)` (×14 in this sample, and it has 2,854 videos at 176
subs/video); Marshal: `BATTLEFIELD 6 BATTLE ROYAL WIN GAMEPLAY PS5PRO(NO
COMMENTARY)`. **Title repetition per game is fine — the catalogue does the work.**
This channel does not need a clever title per upload; it needs volume at a
consistent, searchable pattern.

## 5. Format parameters the data fixes for us

- **Length: 20–36 min** (Marshal 21–24, NoAnnoyingCommentary 22–43, Threatty 22,
  DeeV 22). The K2a-style ~20 min cut is right — and do **not** split into parts.
- **Like rate: 0.7–2.6%**, and no-commentary solo BR sits at **1.2–2.3%**. The
  old "≥0.6%" target is too low; use **≥1%**.
- **Cadence is the variable:** NoAnnoyingCommentary 2,854 videos, NoAnnoyingCommentary Z
  1,345, Isaac4K 3,713 (11.4K subs but **10K–22K views** on plain
  `Battlefield 6: [mode] gameplay (No Commentary)` uploads post past month).
  Mid-size channels win by *volume across modes*, not by one big video.

## 6. Series ranking changes

1. **Battlefield 6 REDSEC Solos — primary.** 9/10 no-commentary, 20–40 min,
   proven floor 100–400 and ceiling 700K+.
2. **Black Ops 7 Zombies — highest-ceiling fallback.** NoAnnoyingCommentary Z
   (66.7K subs) is doing **4,121–80,892 views per map upload, every map, at
   27–370 views/day** — i.e. exactly SooranXD's BO4 plan, but on the current CoD.
   **This makes the BIOS/attestation fix worth real effort, not a side quest.**
3. **Delta Force — demoted.** Only **3/10** results are no-commentary; the
   traction (`I Started From Zero In Solo Delta Force` 257K, `How Solo Players
   Make MILLIONS` 32.9K) belongs to **commentary** channels. Keep it as variety,
   not a growth bet.
4. **PUBG / Arena Breakout** — still unmeasured; bench.
