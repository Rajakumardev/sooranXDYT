# Game-pool ranking — 2026-10-15

**Asked:** the creator's available pool is **BF6 REDSEC · BO4 Zombies · Delta Force
· KF2**, with **KF3** wanted "on sale" and **ECC: REDLINE** (new NetEase Warzone
clone) on the horizon. Which deserve the 3×/week slots?

**Tool:** `youtube` MCP — 4 × `searchVideos` + `getVideoDetails` +
`getChannelStatistics` (≈ 402 units), plus external sources for the two games not
measurable from YouTube alone.

---

## 1. The rule this data reveals

**Demand lives in the *current* keyword — the patch, season, event or launch — not
in the game.** Same game, same format, same channel skill; the only difference is
whether the title names something that is *live right now*:

| Game | Evergreen / stale keyword | Live event-or-season keyword |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KF2** | K1 `Burning Paris Commando (Normal)` = **6 views**; the niche's #1 for that string = 2,774 views in 11 years | `Killing Floor 2 Abandon All Hope: Firebug Solo Airship` — **2,720-sub channel → 36,588 views in 32.9 days = 1,112/day** |
| **BF6** | — | Season 5 (2026-10-13); past-month BF6 no-commentary uploads floor at **5,965** |
| **KF3** | — | Halloween Horrors update live; **14,200-sub channel → 114,049 views** on `Killing Floor 3 Solo Gameplay (No Commentary)` |
| **CoD** | — | BO7 per-map Zombies **4,121–80,892** per upload |
| **ECC: REDLINE** | — | reveal-week videos doing **1,969–49,592 views/day** |

This is the single most actionable finding of the whole review cycle. K1 did not
fail because KF2 is dead — it failed because "Burning Paris · Commando · Normal"
is an eleven-year-old string. **Every upload should name something that changed
this month.**

attached — that is the K1 mistake (6 views).
