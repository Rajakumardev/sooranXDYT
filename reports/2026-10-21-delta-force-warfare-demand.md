## 4. Supply check — the lane is not crowded, because it's not lucrative

`Delta Force gameplay`, **past week**, 40 results: dominated by **mobile** (ApeGod, Vizuff, Razor), **Operations/extraction** (EqualPlays, Streyfs, bbreadman, Aznboi), **Shorts**, and CJK/Vietnamese channels. **Zero** Western PC Warfare no-commentary uploads in that window except Razor's stream VOD and GJ15NILU's one upload today.

So: low competition, because low reward. There is room, not a gold rush.

## 5. Verdict

**Yes, there is demand — but it is second-tier demand, and it is search/map-news demand, not browse-feed demand.**

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does anyone care about Warfare? | **Strongly yes** | 0.8M–4.9M per map trailer; 27K–34K views/day on the current ones |
| Will they watch a 20–35 min no-commentary match? | **Yes, 1K–7K of them** | median 1,993; best 7,132 |
| Is a 20-sub channel's ceiling here? | **300–1,500 on a normal upload, 3K–8K on a map-drop upload** | 324-sub channel → 1,220; 10.2K-sub → 7,132 |
| Is it better than BF6 REDSEC? | **No — roughly 10–15× worse in absolute views** | REDSEC no-commentary median **28,020** (`2026-10-14-phase2-demand-check.md`) vs **1,993** here |
| Is it better than Operations? | **Yes, ~3×** | Delta Force Gameplays: Warfare 1,220 vs Operations 396 |

**Recommendation: keep Delta Force as Series B / filler, but switch the mode from Operations to Warfare, and only fire it on a map/season drop.** No growth slot. This *confirms* the 2026-10-14 "filler only" verdict and refines it: the filler should be Warfare.

## 6. If used, copy-paste packaging

Title formula (mirrors the two proven patterns — Isaac4K's plain one, GJ15NILU's "FIRST LOOK & HIGH KILL"):

```
DELTA FORCE: [MAP NAME] WARFARE GAMEPLAY (NO COMMENTARY)
NEW [MAP NAME] MAP — [N] KILL GAMEPLAY | DELTA FORCE (NO COMMENTARY)
```

- **Length 20–35 min**, one unbroken match, no splitting into parts. Every winner in §2–3 sits at 18:47–34:41.
- **Hook on the map/season, never "Delta Force gameplay."** Live now: The Mog (Sep 2026), Season 12 weapons (AN-94), the UE5 Warfare build, the Detonation 5v5 beta.
- **Name the real result** — `111 KILLS`, `62 KILL Attack Run`. The kill-count titles in §2 got the comments (ont1c: 1.50% comment rate, 6× the no-commentary norm).
- **Don't claim 4K.** Isaac4K and OperatorGaming own the 4K angle; the 3050 Ti (3,962 MB VRAM) cannot honestly deliver it. Compete on map novelty + result, capture 1080p60 high-bitrate per `CONTENT-PLAN.md` §capture.
- Ping-pong it with a 30–45s Short from the same match — the channel's own history says shorts carry the best like ratios (6–9.4%, `AGENTS.md`).

**Do not** run a 10-video Warfare series before BF6 REDSEC Solos is established. The absolute numbers do not support it.

## 7. Caveats

- `searchVideos` `order=viewCount` did **not** sort by views in this session (verified twice — it returned mixed-age relevance results). All rankings here come from `getVideoDetails` view counts, not the search order.
- "The Mog" numbers are 43–60 days old and still climbing; `views/day` for GJ15NILU (165.9) is inflated by recency relative to Isaac4K's 10–11/day.
- Search volume itself is not measurable with this API. Map-trailer views are a **proxy** for interest, not proof of search queries.
- `Delta Force` keyword space is heavily polluted by the real-life US Army unit and by mobile — filter every future query with `Warfare` or `PC`.
