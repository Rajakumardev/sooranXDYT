# SooranXD — Competitive Analysis: FPS vs. "Full-Run / No-Commentary" Niche

- **Generated:** 2026-10-03
- **Question:** Should SooranXD continue FPS (Call of Duty / Shatterline) multiplayer,
  or pivot to the "no-commentary full run / longplay" niche?
- **Method:** 10 targeted `searchVideos` queries (viewCount + recency order) across
  both niches; `getChannelStatistics` on 20 competitor channels;
  `getVideoDetails` on 14 representative videos. All numbers below are from those
  calls.

## TL;DR

**Pivot to the no-commentary full-run / longplay niche, anchored on Risk of Rain 2.**
The FPS multiplayer-match corner SooranXD occupies is a saturated commodity where
micro-channels upload daily for ~0 subscribers. The full-run niche is smaller,
rewards fewer/longer videos, has durable search demand, and — critically — Risk of
Rain 2 no-commentary runs are routinely served by *small* channels (25K–400K views
per video), not entrenched giants.

## 2. FPS multiplayer: saturated commodity

The flagship evidence is Galizma — same format as SooranXD (single CoD multiplayer
match, per-weapon, no commentary):

- `pjGwdeoBk5s` "Kastov 762 | CoD MW2 Multiplayer gameplay (No Commentary)":
  **3,990 views in 1,140 days ≈ 3 views/day**, 2.46% like ratio.
- `lwLl-1TcUt8` "Fennec 45 | CoD MW3 Multiplayer gameplay (No Commentary)"
  (uploaded 2026-10-03): **303 views in 1 day**.

Galizma has 23,900 subs and 4,603 videos and each new match still gets low
hundreds of views. Single-match multiplayer no-commentary does not compound — it
is consumed once and buried.

The **only FPS corner that breaks out** is Warzone solo battle royale:

- Marshal No Commentary `uTShBGgSn3s` "WARZONE SOLO SNIPER MORS GAMEPLAY PS5
  PRO (No Commentary)": **2,205,788 views**, 4,961 views/day after 445 days.
- Marshal `Qop1sH70nHI` "CoD Warzone 3 Solo Rebirth PS5 (No Commentary)":
  **2,560,900 views**.
- MKIceAndFire BO7 campaign `e5YLN0-tqWk`: 1,955,011 views (but that's a
  big-budget full campaign, not a match).

Note the winning FPS titles carry **quality/context hooks** ("PS5 PRO", "SNIPER
MORS") and publish **full runs of a whole BR match**, not a slice of TDM. SooranXD's
"TDM / Domination / Hardpoint single match" format sits in the weakest corner of
an already-commoditised space.

Also: Shatterline is effectively dead. The most recent no-commentary Shatterline
walkthrough (`KT2XoYvFOY4`, Sep 2025) has **4,404 views over 370 days ≈ 12/day**.
Do not build on it.

Key contrasts vs. FPS:

1. **Small channels win here.** No commentary dog (8.5K subs), BlueTricity, Fake
   Acrid, Long Play TV (2.7K subs) — these are not giants, and their full runs get

2. **Like ratios are 2–4× higher** (0.46%–1.74% vs. Galizma's ~2.5% on a much

3. **Videos are evergreen.** A full-run title like "Risk of Rain 2 gameplay part 1

   A TDM match has essentially no long-tail.
4. **Search demand is game-name driven and stable** ("risk of rain 2 no
   commentary", "risk of rain 2 full run", "[game] full playthrough no
   commentary") — a repeatable keyword formula across every game.

## 4. Risk of Rain 2 specifically

- The niche is **not owned by a giant.** Top results are old/small channels and
  the format is under-supplied relative to the player base.
- The strongest single asset is "No commentary dog" part 1 at **386,953 views**
  (0.46% LR) — proof of ceiling.
- Mid-tier runs (fgw, Fake Acrid, BlueTricity) land **25K–40K**, which is
  **2,000×+ SooranXD's current single video** (11 views).
- SooranXD's existing upload (`eXFAFQxc6rc`) already uses the right *format*
  (13-minute run, correct tags/description) — it is failing on **packaging**
  (thumbnail has no hook) and **zero distribution**, not on niche choice.

## 5. Recommendation

**Primary path — pivot to "no-commentary full run / longplay", starting with
Risk of Rain 2.**

Rationale, from the data:

- Highest ceiling-per-effort: small channels get 25K–400K on single full runs;
  SooranXD's current FPS format gets single/double digits.
- Lowest competition floor: RoR2 no-commentary is served by sub-10K-sub channels,
  not MKIceAndFire.
- Evergreen: each upload is a durable search asset, unlike a match.
- Fastest validation: SooranXD can test it with **zero new game cost** using the
  RoR2 upload already on the channel.

**Execution for the primary path**

1. **Commit to a run of 20–30 uploads before judging** (the niche rewards
   catalogue depth; Long Play TV needed ~93).
2. **Title formula:** `[Game] [Full Run / Full Playthrough] — [character/difficulty]
   (No Commentary)` — under 60 chars, game name first. Examples:
   - "Risk of Rain 2 Full Run — Commando, Monsoon (No Commentary)"
   - "Risk of Rain 2: Mithrix Kill, No Commentary (Full Run)"
   - "Risk of Rain 2 Railgunner Run — No Commentary"
3. **One game per series**, then expand to adjacent roguelikes/indies with the
   same proven format (Hades, Dead Cells, Enter the Gungeon, Rain World — all
   appear repeatedly in no-commentary searches with small-channel wins).
4. **Package the first 60 seconds** — even no-commentary runs need a cold open
   (best fight / boss / item combo) to survive the retention cliff.
5. **Use chapters** for the run (item pickups, boss fights, finale) — the niche's
   long videos live or die on navigability.

**Secondary/optional — Warzone solo, if staying in FPS.**
If you want to keep an FPS foot in the door, do **Warzone solo no-commentary**,
not TDM/Domination:
- Proven breakout: Marshal's solo Warzone videos do 2.2M–2.5M views.
- Hooks must include platform + weapon/quality ("PS5 PRO", "SNIPER MORS", "SOLO
  REBIRTH").
- But it is crowded and trend-dependent, and SooranXD's catalogue is on sunset
  titles (MW 2019, BOCW). Treat this as a test, not the foundation.

**Do not** continue the current single-match TDM/Domination no-commentary format,
and **do not** make more Shatterline content.

## 6. What to measure (30-day / 20-upload test)

| Metric | FPS baseline (current) | Target for full-run path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Views/video (30-day) | ~10–50 | ≥ 300 |
| Views/video/day | ~0–3 | ≥ 10 |
| Like ratio | 1–9% (volatile) | ≥ 0.6% (niche norm) |
| Subscriber growth | ~0 | ≥ +20 in 90 days |
| Evergreen check | flat | keep climbing after 30 days |

If after 20–30 full-run uploads the channel is still under ~300 views/video, the
bottleneck is packaging/distribution, not niche — revisit thumbnails and
promotion before switching again.

### How this changes the plan

This is better news than a pure RoR2 pivot: KF2 and L4D2 are **shooters**, so
they preserve channel continuity with the existing FPS identity. The channel
positioning becomes **"no-commentary full runs of co-op / survival shooters"**
(L4D2, KF2, RoR2, Deep Rock, Helldivers), which is coherent and evergreen.

**Recommended priority order:**

1. **Risk of Rain 2** — cheapest test, format already proven by small channels,
   one upload already live.
2. **Killing Floor 2** — highest proven efficiency (130 subs/video) and a
   copyable formula; only 364 videos in the incumbent's catalogue leaves room.
3. **Left 4 Dead 2** — biggest evergreen ceiling, but treat as the third series
   because its search intent is diluted and its floor is crowded.

Rule: run **one game series at a time** for 20–30 uploads so the channel and
algorithm can read a clear signal. Do not interleave all three from day one.

## 7. Caveats

- `searchVideos` does not return per-video view counts; the video-level views
  above come from targeted `getVideoDetails` calls on the top results, so they
  are representative rather than exhaustive.
- The longplay niche's giants (Gamer's Little Playground, Shirrako) are
  full-game-movie channels with decade-old authority; SooranXD competes with the
  *small* tier (Long Play TV, fgw, Fake Acrid), which is winnable.
- `findConsistentOutlierChannels` was unavailable (needs MongoDB), so outlier
  detection was done via search + manual stats.
