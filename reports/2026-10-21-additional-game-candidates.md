# Additional game candidates — does anything else clear the bar?

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · companion to `2026-10-21-game-pool-rerank-after-hardware-wall.md`

**The bar a game has to clear:**
1. Runs on **RTX 3050 Ti Laptop / 3,962 MB VRAM / 15.4 GB RAM / 1080p**.
2. **No ranked 5v5** — casual, self-paced, no team dependency.
3. One self-contained run per upload, with a **checkable result**.
4. A **no-commentary lane with small-channel proof** — not just a big creator's numbers.
5. Ideally nameable against something **live now**.

**Tool:** `youtube` MCP — 10 × `searchVideos` (1,000 units) + `getVideoDetails` (≈56 IDs) + `getChannelStatistics` (23 channels) + web spec checks.

### Why this clears the bar
- **Hardware: trivial.** BF1 minimum is a **GTX 660 2 GB / 8 GB RAM** (2016 game). Your
  3050 Ti runs it at 1080p high with headroom. No compromise, no 720p, no excuses.
- **Format: exact fit.** Conquest / Operations = a 20–46 min self-contained match,
  64 players, **no rank ladder, no teammates required**, no aim-grind. Win/loss + score
  is the result.
- **Small-channel proof: Isaac4K.** 11.5K subs, **13 BF1 uploads in 7 weeks**
  (Sep 2 → Oct 18), running 2,433–12,316 views. His channel average is 2,177/video, so
  **BF1 is performing at or above his whole-channel average** — he is deliberately
  leaning into it, which is the strongest possible "this is working" signal.
- **A live keyword exists right now:** the 10th anniversary (Oct 2026). Note how his
  post-anniversary uploads are moving at **700–1,165 views/day** versus his September
  ones at 157/day. The spike is real and it is happening this week.
- **Nobody is combining "anniversary" with "no commentary."** jackfrags and
  TheBrokenMachine own the anniversary *commentary/clips* angle. The silent lane is
  unclaimed.
- **The mode keyword matters:** his single best BF1 upload is **Back to Basics
  Operations (12,316)** — the bolt-action-only mode — not plain Conquest. That's a
  distinctive, under-used hook.

### Caveats
- **The window is weeks, not months.** BF1's anniversary traffic decays; after that it
  reverts to evergreen (Isaac4K still gets 2.4–8K on plain titles, so evergreen works —
  just slower).
- **Below ~1K subs the floor is brutal:** Bolt Action Broadcast (144 subs) gets 35–75,
  MarsULTOR (1,060 subs) gets single digits. You will not skip the climb, but the 10K-sub
  tier is demonstrably comfortable.
- **Verify server population in your region** before committing a week to it. BF1 is
  nine years old with a shrinking but loyal playerbase; Isaac4K's Oct 2026 cadence
  suggests healthy queues at least in his region.
- BF1 is bought once (frequently $5–10 on sale; also on EA Play).

## 8. Titles (copy-paste)

```
BATTLEFIELD 1: BACK TO BASICS OPERATIONS — 10TH ANNIVERSARY (NO COMMENTARY)
BATTLEFIELD 1: OPERATIONS GAMEPLAY — FULL MATCH (NO COMMENTARY)
BATTLEFIELD 1: CONQUEST GAMEPLAY (NO COMMENTARY)
PUBG PC : SOLO FPP GAMEPLAY IN [MAP] | TOP 1 [N] KILLS (NO COMMENTARY)
```
- `BACK TO BASICS` is the differentiator: Isaac4K's best BF1 upload (**12,316**) used it,
  his plain Operations uploads did 2.4–7.3K.
- Do **not** claim 4K. HellGhost brands `NO HUD … (4K)`; you can compete on **NO HUD**
  (which costs you nothing) but not on resolution.
- The anniversary phrase has a shelf life — use it while it is true, then drop it.

## 9. On a *second* channel

Don't split yet. Two channels means two cold starts and half the upload cadence on each,
and this niche rewards volume per format (Xeon: 309 videos; NoAnnoyingCommentary: 2,854).

If you do split later, split by **format**, not by game:
- **Channel A (main):** solo-PvP BR / large-scale — PUBG FPP, BF1.
- **Channel B:** PvE runs — Ready or Not, KF2 events, BO4.
Mixing a silent BF1 match with a Ready or Not mission on one channel is fine and cheaper;
splitting only makes sense once one lane is consistently clearing 1K+ per upload.

## 10. Caveats

- `searchVideos` `order=viewCount` unreliable again; all numbers from `getVideoDetails`.
- BF1's anniversary numbers (jackfrags 314K/day) are **commentary** content — they measure
  interest in the game, not demand for silent gameplay. The silent numbers are Isaac4K's
  2.4–12.3K.
- Helldivers 2 / Apex / Fortnite conclusions rest on one incumbent each; treat them as
  "not proven", not "proven dead".
- Battlefield 1 server population in the Asia region is unverified — check before
  recording a week of content.
