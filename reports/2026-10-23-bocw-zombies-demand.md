## 4. Format notes

- **Outbreak is the wedge.** It produced every good small-channel BOCW number: SHERDO's 2–6K,
  [Knightime85]'s 709. It's open-world, self-paced, PvE — no round-survival skill wall, which suits
  a day-job schedule. Round-based maps (Die Maschine, Mauer der Toten) are dominated by the 67K leader
  and collapse to 45–179 for everyone else.
- **Outbreak runs are long:** 1h04–1h17 in the sampled videos. That's a different cut from KF2's 20–30 min.
- **Two proven hooks:** nostalgia framing (`5.5 Years Later`, `Still Fun in 2026?`, `Nostalgie pur`),
  and graphics showcase (`RTX 4090 Ultra`). You can only claim the first.
- **Map names are evergreen** — no live keyword exists (BO7 is the current CoD), so this is
  catalogue work, not a spike play.

## 6. Verify before committing disk

1. **Does BOCW launch?** It's a standalone Battle.net/Steam client, not CoD HQ — but confirm it starts
   and reaches the main menu before re-downloading. If the attestation block follows the Battle.net
   account rather than the client, this whole plan dies at step one.
2. **Install zombies-only** if the client offers mode selection — it's much smaller than the full package.
3. **Check Outbreak is still populated** (it's solo-able, so population matters less than for MP).

## 7. Caveats

- BOCW's leader numbers (8–9K) are 43–44 days old and still climbing at ~200/day; they are not a
  ceiling for a new channel.
- SHERDO's 2–6K is the one mid-small datapoint and may not generalise — treat 50–200 as the
  realistic starting band.
- 7-sub channel → 709 views is an outlier, not a floor. One good hook can do it; most won't.
- No 2026 content updates for BOCW, so titles cannot use the "live now" rule — this lane lives or
  dies on map names + framing.
